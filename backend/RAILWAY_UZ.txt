RAILWAY UCHUN QISQA YO'RIQNOMA

1) Railwayda yangi service oching.
2) Root Directory sifatida backend ni tanlang.
3) Volume ulang va mount path ni /data qiling.
4) Variables ga .env.example dagi qiymatlarni kiriting.
5) FRONTEND_URL ga Netlify domeningizni yozing.
6) Start Command kerak bo'lsa: gunicorn wsgi:app --workers 1 --threads 4 --timeout 120

Backend ishlari:
- API: /api/submit
- Admin: /admin
- Excel yuklash: /api/download/excel
- Telegram bot polling ham shu backendda ishlaydi.

Railway Variables ichida DATA_DIR=/data qilib yozing. Lokal ishga tushirishda esa DATA_DIR=data bo'lishi mumkin.
